Digital Public Infrastructure, built for people.

Druk.help is a Bhutan-based initiative to advance open-source technology and Digital Public Goods for Bhutan and the world.

We help governments, communities, developers, and public-interest organizations adopt proven Digital Public Goods, build new open technologies where gaps exist, and deploy them sustainably at population scale.

Our ambition is simple: use technology that belongs to everyone to build public systems that work for everyone.

01

From Bhutan, for the world.

Countries everywhere face similar challenges in healthcare, education, public administration, climate resilience, and access to essential services.

Many of the digital systems needed to address these challenges already exist as open-source software and Digital Public Goods. Yet adopting them successfully requires much more than installing software.

It requires local capacity, implementation support, interoperability, governance, community ownership, and long-term stewardship.

Druk.help exists to build that capability in Bhutan.

We want Bhutan to become not only a user of Digital Public Goods, but also a creator, contributor, and global implementation partner for open digital infrastructure.

02

What we do

01

Adopt

Bring proven Digital Public Goods to Bhutan

We identify open-source platforms that can strengthen public services in Bhutan and work with government, communities, and partners to adapt them for local needs.

Rather than rebuilding systems that already exist, we help Bhutan benefit from technology developed and proven around the world.

02

Deploy

Turn open-source software into working public infrastructure

Technology alone does not create impact.

Druk.help supports implementation—from architecture and localisation to integration, hosting, training, deployment, and long-term operations.

Our goal is to help Digital Public Goods move from repositories to real public services.

03

Build

Create Digital Public Goods from Bhutan

Where important gaps remain, we bring together Bhutanese developers, institutions, domain experts, and global open-source communities to build new solutions.

Projects developed through Druk.help should be open by default and designed so that other countries can reuse and improve them.

04

Contribute

Strengthen the global DPG ecosystem

Every implementation creates knowledge.

We contribute improvements, documentation, implementation playbooks, integrations, standards, and software back to upstream open-source communities wherever possible.

Bhutan should benefit from the global commons—and contribute to it.

05

Enable

Help other countries deploy Digital Public Goods

Over time, Druk.help aims to build a Bhutan-based community of engineers, implementers, designers, and public-interest technologists capable of supporting DPG deployments beyond Bhutan.

This creates an opportunity for Bhutan to become a trusted centre for Digital Public Infrastructure implementation and stewardship.

05

Why Bhutan?

༄༅།།Bhutan has the opportunity to take a different approach to digital transformation.

Instead of becoming dependent on closed, proprietary systems, the country can build on open standards, open-source software, shared knowledge, and Digital Public Goods.

Its scale makes Bhutan an exceptional environment to demonstrate how interoperable, citizen-centred public infrastructure can be implemented across an entire country.

And its tradition of measuring progress beyond purely economic outcomes creates a natural foundation for technology designed around public value rather than technology for its own sake.

Druk.help wants to help turn that opportunity into reality.

Global DPG communities

Projects and organizations sharing technology, standards, experience, and knowledge.

Institutions developing local talent and generating evidence around digital transformation.

Organizations supporting sustainable public-interest technology and capacity building.

Other countries

Governments and implementation partners that can learn from Bhutan—and work with Bhutanese teams to deploy Digital Public Goods elsewhere.

07

Our principles

Open by default

We believe public-interest technology should be transparent, reusable, and collaborative wherever possible.

Public value first

Technology is a means to improve people’s lives—not an outcome by itself.

Build with, not for

Systems should be developed with the institutions, communities, and people who will use them.

Interoperability over silos

Open standards allow different systems and organizations to work together.

Local ownership

Sustainable digital infrastructure requires local people who can operate, improve, and govern it.

Global commons

What Bhutan learns and builds should strengthen the broader Digital Public Goods ecosystem.

08 · Our ambition

Bhutan as a global contributor to Digital Public Goods.

We imagine a future where Bhutan:

  • runs critical public infrastructure using world-class open technologies;
  • develops Bhutanese engineers and institutions with deep DPG expertise;
  • creates new Digital Public Goods used internationally;
  • contributes improvements back to global open-source projects;
  • becomes a reference environment for responsible Digital Public Infrastructure; and
  • supports other countries adopting open technology for public good.

Adopt globally.Build locally.Contribute openly.
