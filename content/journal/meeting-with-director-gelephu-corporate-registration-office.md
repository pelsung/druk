## What we saw

We met Yeshi Choki at GMC today and shared the philosophy, vision and design of the knowledge transfer program, how knowledge and learning were transferred from mentors to the Pelsups over the past two weeks.

It was learned that Yeshi had envisioned a similar initiative even before Pelsung took shape. She was gratified to learn about the Knowledge Transfer Program and its collaboration with Pelsups, and expressed her appreciation for the work being carried out through Pelsung.


She aligned the civico protocol & DPG model and suggested that the non-profit structure used by religious institutions - Public Company Limited by Guarantee shall be the best fit. She shared two contacts of corporate service providers (CSPs) approved for registration and the application fees was stated as 500 USD.

The registration process is expected to take approximately two weeks, but prior to that,  GFSO in-principle licensing needs to be completed and then the required capital will be determined.

## Personal reflection

**Why did we come for the meeting?** As Sanjay shared the vision behind druk.help, touching on CIVICO and the DPG model, Ms.Yeshi Choki genuinely shared our perspective. Her suggestion of a Public Company Limited by Guarantee aligned well with our vision. Our primary interest is serving public welfare, not for commercial gain, like the religious institutions using the same structure. It is reassuring that GCRO resonates with the vision of our initiative.

**What happened?** There was a quiet sense of fulfillment while walking out of the office after the meeting. The path forward feels clear and made lighter by the presence of another, appreciating our vision and mission.

**What’s next?** Varun will meet CSP (Corporate Service Provider to carry registration forward, then we submit our Expression of Interest.
