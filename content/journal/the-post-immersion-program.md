## What we saw

Writing

After completing the three-month immersion program, which ran from March to the first week of June, Pelsung announced its first-ever Post-Immersion Programs for Pelsups from Cohorts 1 to 3. Among the various programs introduced was the Digital Public Goods (DPG) and Free and Open Source Software (FOSS) Post-Immersion Program, designed to provide further learning and practical exposure in the field.

The Digital Public Goods and FOSS Post-Immersion Program was announced, and a total of 31 applicants applied for the program. The applicants were invited to the first orientation day on 30 August at the Pelsung office, where some attended in person while most joined online. The mentors introduced the concepts of Digital Public Goods (DPG) and Free and Open Source Software (FOSS) and explained the purpose of the program. We were then given a few days to form teams consisting of one health worker, one communication person, and one developer. A total of three teams were formed, after which two teams were selected to participate in the first Knowledge Sharing Program.

## Personal reflection

**Why did we come for the meeting?** We came to understand the purpose of the program, the importance of teamwork, and the responsibility we were preparing to take on. Initially, we expected to build a website to support the healthcare system and gain technical exposure through the program.

**What happened?** However, we began to realise that the work extended far beyond technology. It was about understanding real challenges on the ground, listening to the needs of communities and healthcare workers, and exploring how digital solutions could address those needs meaningfully.

**What’s next?** The first orientation gave us a glimpse of what lay ahead. We began to understand the purpose of the program, the importance of teamwork, and the responsibility we were preparing to take on. We looked forward to working and collaborating with fellow Pelsups from different backgrounds to take the initiative forward and contribute towards meaningful change in Bhutan’s healthcare system.
