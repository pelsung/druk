## What we saw

We worked on developing the Story and Journal sections for Druk.Help. These sections are designed to give people a space to share their experiences, thoughts, reflections, and stories related to healthcare. The aim is to make sharing simple and accessible, while also creating a place where others can read, learn, and connect with different experiences.

## Personal reflection

**Why did we come for the meeting?** We came together to discuss how the Story and Journal sections of Druk.Help should work and what kind of content people could share. We wanted to create a simple and welcoming space where users could express their experiences and reflections about healthcare.

**What happened?** We discussed the difference between the two sections and how they can be useful to the community. The Story section can be used for sharing personal experiences and meaningful healthcare stories, while the Journal section can be used for regular reflections, thoughts, learning experiences, and updates. We focused on making both sections easy to use and easy for others to read.

**What’s next?** The next step is to test both the Story and Journal sections and make sure the process of writing and sharing is simple for users. We will also continue improving how the content is organised and displayed so that people can easily explore different stories and journal entries and learn from the experiences shared by the community.
