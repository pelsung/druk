## What we saw

8 Pelsup participants, the Pelsung ED, mentors, and professionals gathered for the opening of the program. The program began with prayers, followed by a discussion on Pelsung Grantham.The Kerala Care dashboard working live and its advanced features. At the end of the day, we chose a name for our website: Drukhelp.

## Personal reflection

**Why did we come for the meeting?** Our journey with Drukhelp began at Hotel Phuntsho Pelri, where we gathered for an overview of the programme, its purpose, and the way forward. We came with hopes, aspirations, and a shared desire to contribute to something meaningful, particularly in improving healthcare services and strengthening community-based palliative care in Bhutan. As we listened to the vision of the programme, we began to imagine the possibilities and the impact our collective efforts could create.

**What happened?** The meeting gave us an initial understanding of the programme and its intended direction. It was an opportunity to discuss ideas, understand our responsibilities, and explore how we could move forward together. As the programme progressed, however, we began to recognise that turning a shared vision into reality would require more than enthusiasm. It would require clear roles, effective communication, collaboration, and a deeper understanding of the needs on the ground.

**What’s next?** We begin this journey carrying many hopes and aspirations for what Drukhelp could become. Moving forward, the challenge is to translate those aspirations into practical and meaningful action. We need to strengthen our understanding of the programme, identify the gaps that need attention, and develop solutions that respond to the real needs of patients, healthcare workers, and communities.
