## What we saw

Our team, together with our mentor Varun, met with Mrs Yangdon, Senior Program Officer of the Cancer program  at the Ministry of Health.

## Personal reflection

**Why did we come for the meeting?** We introduced our project and shared what we are building. We also wanted to understand the workflow issues currently faced in the field and to learn about the plans and programs that lie ahead. The guidance, support and collaboration of the Ministry of Health are a crucial pillar of our project, and this meeting was an important step toward building that partnership.

**What happened?** We had a brief discussion on the current workflow issues. We also talked about upcoming plans and programs. The pilot project is set to begin in December. The discussions expanded further on the clinical form integration and the dashboard.

**What’s next?** In the next meeting, we will go deeper into the dashboard and the clinical forms, . These discussions will help us understand how the dashboard can fit into the existing workflow and address the issues currently faced in the field. They will also help us refine the clinical forms so that they are practical and ready for deployment. With the pilot project set to begin in December, we are looking forward to continuing the conversation and moving one step closer to bringing Druk Help into Reality.
