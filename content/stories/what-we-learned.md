Our field visit to palliative care was an emotional and meaningful experience. Before the visit, we had a limited understanding of what palliative care involved. Listening to the experiences of patients, caregivers, and healthcare workers helped us see the challenges families face during difficult stages of illness. What stayed with us most was the importance of simply being present, listening, and making patients feel respected and cared for
